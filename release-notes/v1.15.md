## What's new in 1.15

- **Flip labels:** with a second map blended in, the ⇄ Labels button (or the X key) swaps the markers and place names to the blended map. Cross-game blends (ALttP, ALBW, EoW) show the other game's markers lined up on this map.
- **Sharp deep zoom** on 28 overworld maps, including TotK surface and depths, Wind Waker, Phantom Hourglass, Minish Cap, the Oracles and ALttP, and on the cross-game blends.
- **Smoother zooming**, blends that fade in correctly at any strength, and markers that stay on top when zoomed in.
