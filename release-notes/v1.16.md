## What's new in 1.16

- **Smoother zooming on busy maps:** with lots of layers switched on (for example every BotW/TotK layer, about 2,700 markers), zooming and panning are several times faster. Markers settle to their size when you let go.
- **Instant checklist ticks:** marking items found in the Checklist view no longer redraws the whole sidebar, even with every group open.
- **Tidier credits:** the in-app credits describe where the base maps come from, and the progress-sync note now simply points to Export and Import.

This is a community-maintained project now; see [HANDOVER.md](https://github.com/diggdirk04/zelda-maps/blob/main/HANDOVER.md) if you'd like to carry it on.
